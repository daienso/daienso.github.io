Our Consulting Process

01

Understanding Business Needs

The process initiates with the presentation of the problems and requirements by the enterprise, followed by a collaborative discussion and establishment of the key steps and required resources, concluding with an agreements on scoping.

This step may take 1-2 free hours meeting.

02

Initial Consulting Contract (1-5 Cycles)

In accordance with the finalized scope of work, we establish a short time period pre-study/assessment contract.

Some of the advisory topics include (i) feasibility analysis and assessment, (ii) High-level architecture design, (iii) Evaluations of solution candidates.

03

Scaling for long term modernization

We will then move forward with project-based or regular consulting to drive continuous modernization and transformation if required.
